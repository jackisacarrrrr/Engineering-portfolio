## Problem Statement
 Plastic waste has become a significant problem that directly impacts both humans but also the enviroment. The improper disposal of plastic can harm aninmals, futhermore it can enter the soil and streams 
 breaking down into microplastics.

 ### Adressing the problem
 Fayetteville High school can not solve the global problem, but we can address this a a local level: The goal being to lower the enviromental impact of plastic in Fayetteville

 ## Project Goal

 Design, construct, and program a functional plastic recycling system that can effiecently turn plastic into usable material.

 ### Approching the goal

 The machine will not be able to deal with all the plastic in Fayetteville. It should be able to produce enough material that it makes the use of the machine worth the investment. 
 We can mesure this by looking at the inital measures of sucess. These are:

  - Plastic is processed consistently
  - The system is reliable and produces a useful output
  - The ammunt of plastic diverted from waste to the machine can be measured
  - The time it takes process the plastic is resonable
  - The energy usage is resonable compared to the production of plastic

  ### The Criteria
  The will be used as a checklist of sorts. The criteria is what must be done for the machine to be operated.
    
  - Process selected types of plastic effiecntly
  - Turn plastic into usable material
  - Be simple to operate without specialized training
  - Easy to maintain and clean
  - Be safe enough for high schoolers to operate
  - Be affordable < $1000 to build
  - Include a proper sorting system
  - Mimize exposure to plastic fumes and microplastis


## Constraints
  We must also considered the constraints put apon the project. Failure to comply with these constraints will result in the project failing. These constraints are:

- Plastic must be properly sorted before the process
- Melting plastic can release fumes and particles (ventilation required)
- Any part of the machine that has moving parts or heating elements must be properly monitored and labled
- The who process should be able to be completed in a 90-minute class period
- The project must stay withing the listed budget, being made of low cost parts
- The equipment should fit within a small area, no more then 7*7 Feet
- The machine must use standard 120V or 240V AC power, and no more than 10 AMPs to avoid shorting a breaker
- All eletrical equipment requires appropriate protection
