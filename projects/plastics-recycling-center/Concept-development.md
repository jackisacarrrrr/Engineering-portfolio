## Brainstorming
We must begin the inital design process of brainstorming each part of the machine and how each part will work.

### What do we need to do, and what machines will we need?
As stated in the design breif the machine must, shred, melt, and extrude the plastic into a mold

#### Mechanisms
Sub-mechanisms needed are:
- Cleaner
- Shredder
- Heating elements
- Extrusions system

These will all be combined to make the full plastic processing machine (cleaner can be made as a seperate non-mechanical machine)

### Safety
Safety is a top prioty for this project some following safety precautions are:
- Phyiscal blocks and guards
- Eletronic detection systems
  - Programable sensors
  - Eletric capatience systems
- Warning signs
- Proper ventilation

### Method of production
This is the process the plastic follows as it goes through the machine
1. Plastic is organized by type and cleaned
2. Plastic is shredded into small particles
3. Plastic is moved to a melting chamber
4. Plastic is melted
5. Plastic is exturded into a mold
6. Mold is removed and the final product is removed.

#### End product
The end product must be able to be molded via extusion, this means it has to be a simple shape with minimal ammounts of sharp turns as the presure of the liqid plastic will not be high
We decided to cast plastic beams as they are strong and multiuse.
